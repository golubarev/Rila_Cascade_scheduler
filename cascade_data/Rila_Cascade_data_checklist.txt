================================================================
 CONCEPTS TO KEEP IN MIND  (affect how data is structured)
================================================================
* Cascade coupling: upstream releases become downstream inflows
  after a delay. Each reservoir's balance depends on its neighbor.

* Water value: maximizing only today's revenue drains reservoirs to
  zero. We need a planning horizon or an end-of-day target level so
  stored water is valued for tomorrow too.

2. Build the optimization assuming inflows are KNOWN (or held at
   recent values) — get scheduling working end to end.
3. Add a real inflow forecast (snowmelt / weather driven) later.
